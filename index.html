1. Introduction

Welcome to Efficient, developed by AZHORA. Efficient is a personal productivity and planning application designed to help users manage plans, tasks, reminders, focus sessions, health routines and personal productivity.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Efficient processes, how it is used, where it is stored, and when it may be shared with third-party service providers.

Efficient follows a local-first approach. Most personal application information is stored on your device rather than on our own cloud servers.

2. Information We Process

2.1 Personal Profile Information

You may optionally provide:

  • Name
  • Age
  • Date of birth

This information helps personalize the application experience. If you do not provide a name, the application may use the default display name "User". Profile information is stored locally on your device.

2.2 Plans, Tasks and Notes

Efficient processes information you enter, including:

  • Task and plan titles
  • Categories and priorities
  • Dates, times and recurring schedules
  • Notes and descriptions
  • Task completion history
  • Reminder preferences

This information is stored in the application's local database and is used to provide planning and productivity features.

2.3 Health and Wellness Information

Depending on the features you use, Efficient may process:

  • Water intake records
  • Sleep records
  • Exercise and movement activities
  • Meal records
  • Personal health routines
  • Daily and weekly goals
  • Progress records and streaks

This information is used to calculate personal progress and display health-related insights. Health records are stored locally on your device.

Efficient is not intended to provide medical diagnosis or replace professional medical advice.

2.4 Focus Mode Information

Efficient may process:

  • Focus session duration
  • Focus presets and break durations
  • Linked tasks
  • Session start and completion times
  • Focus session history

This information is stored locally and used to provide focus timers, session controls and productivity statistics.

2.5 Reminder and Notification Information

Efficient may process:

  • Reminder dates and times
  • Notification preferences
  • Reminder identifiers
  • Selected notification or alarm sounds
  • Custom ringtone file references

This information supports scheduled reminders and notifications on your device. Efficient may request Android notification, alarm and related permissions where necessary.

2.6 Offline Artificial Intelligence

Efficient may provide AI-powered planning and productivity assistance using an AI model that runs locally on your device.

  • Relevant information is processed locally.
  • AI prompts and planning information are not intentionally transmitted to an external AI API for inference.
  • AI suggestions are presented for your review.
  • You decide whether to accept and save suggested plans.
  • AI-generated information is not automatically saved without your confirmation.

Downloading an AI model may require an internet connection. The model hosting provider may process technical information associated with downloading the file, such as network requests and IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. Once downloaded, supported AI inference can operate offline.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Efficient is used to:

  • Create and manage plans, tasks and notes.
  • Maintain recurring schedules and completion history.
  • Schedule reminders and notifications.
  • Personalize the application experience.
  • Track health and wellness routines.
  • Manage Focus Mode sessions.
  • Calculate productivity statistics.
  • Generate offline AI suggestions.
  • Manage Premium subscriptions and purchases.
  • Maintain application functionality and security.

AZHORA does not use personal information for advertising purposes through Efficient.

4. Data Storage and Security

Efficient uses local device storage for its primary planning and productivity features.

Information such as profile details, tasks, notes, health records, focus history and reminder settings is stored in the application's private local database or application storage.

Efficient does not currently provide its own cloud account or cloud synchronization service for this information.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by the application. However, no electronic storage method is completely secure.

5. Subscriptions and Third-Party Services

Efficient offers optional Premium subscription features through Google Play Billing, with subscription management supported by RevenueCat.

5.1 Google Play

Google Play may process:

  • Subscription product information
  • Purchase and transaction records
  • Payment status
  • Renewal, cancellation and refund information

Google Play handles payment processing. Efficient does not directly collect or store your complete credit card or banking details.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.2 RevenueCat

RevenueCat supports subscription management, purchase verification and entitlement tracking.

RevenueCat may process:

  • Application user identifiers
  • Product and subscription identifiers
  • Purchase and transaction information
  • Subscription status and entitlement information
  • Relevant technical information

This information helps verify purchases, manage Premium access and restore subscriptions.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

5.3 Model Hosting Provider

If you download the offline AI model, the hosting provider may process technical information required to deliver the model file. Downloading the model does not mean your AI prompts or personal planning information are automatically sent to that provider.

6. Information Sharing

AZHORA does not sell or rent your personal information.

We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics in the current application configuration.

Information may be processed by third-party services where necessary to provide features you choose to use, including Google Play subscription processing, RevenueCat subscription management and AI model downloads.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Most personal information created within Efficient remains on your device until you delete it or remove the application data.

You may modify or delete information using the available application features.

You can also clear application data or uninstall Efficient. This may permanently remove locally stored information if it has not been backed up.

Google Play and RevenueCat may retain subscription and transaction records independently according to their policies and legal obligations.

Uninstalling Efficient does not automatically cancel an active subscription. You must manage or cancel your subscription through Google Play.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on available application features, you may:

  • Choose whether to provide optional profile information.
  • Edit or delete profile details.
  • Add, edit or delete plans and notes.
  • Manage health tracking records.
  • Change reminder and notification settings.
  • Enable or disable available AI features.
  • Manage Premium subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Uninstall the application to remove locally stored application information.

You may contact AZHORA with privacy-related questions.

9. Application Permissions

Depending on the features you enable and your Android version, Efficient may request:

  • Notification permission for reminders.
  • Alarm-related permissions for scheduled reminders.
  • Vibration permission for notification and alarm feedback.
  • Foreground service permissions for supported Focus Mode functionality.
  • Access to a user-selected audio file for custom reminder sounds.

These permissions support the relevant application features. You can manage available permissions through your Android device settings.

10. Children's Privacy

Efficient is a general productivity application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children through an account registration system.

If you believe personal information has been provided inappropriately, please contact AZHORA so that the concern can be reviewed.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

AZHORA may update this Privacy Policy when application features, third-party services or applicable legal requirements change.

The updated version will be made available through the application or the official Privacy Policy webpage.
